# Novel Session — Open & Close

## Open (before touching prose)

1. Read the newest entry in `story-state/session-log.md` — especially its
   "Next:" line and any open questions.
2. Skim the ledger headers (current relationship standings, open promises count,
   last reader-experience rows). Headers, not full reads.
3. State **"where we are" in three lines**: what was last written, what was last
   decided, what today is for.
4. Confirm today's single target with the writer. One target. Sessions with three
   goals finish none.

If the last entry flagged something ("12.3 has no Change entry"), resolve or
consciously defer it before new drafting — flags rot.

## Close (always, even short sessions)

Append to `session-log.md`, newest on top, ≤10 lines:

- **Wrote:** scenes/chapters touched, draft status
- **Decided:** each decision WITH its why — decisions are what future sessions forget
- **State touched:** which ledgers got deltas
- **Flagged:** anything left broken on purpose
- **Open question:** what's unresolved in the writer's head
- **Next:** the single concrete step, with its target reader-feeling
  ("draft 13.1 — dread 4, question: is the meeting a trap?")

Then `git commit` with a message that matches the Wrote line. Optionally log the
word count for momentum tracking.

## Rules

- Never end without the "Next:" line — it is tomorrow's two-minute start.
- The log records decisions, not feelings about progress. No self-scolding entries;
  they poison rereads.
- If the session changed canon (a fact, a name, a date), the bible gets the edit NOW,
  not "later".
