Project Gutenberg boilerplate and producer credits stripped per PG's
trademark license (the underlying texts carry no copyright). Provenance
is recorded per work below; four assembled/extracted files also carry a
provenance note at the top of the file itself. Acquired 2026-08-12 via

domain or open license. If a work's status can't be verified, it gets
linked in `../sources/craft-sources.md`, not stored.

## Caveats

- **wells--the-technique-of-the-mystery-story.txt** is assembled from a chapter-by-chapter blog transcription (Jekyll posts). The transcriber's chapter numbering is inconsistent (duplicated labels 22/23/26; labels 9/16/25 absent), so section order is best-effort and the text may lack a few chapters of the 1913 original. Each section header carries the transcription's original chapter label.
- **polti--the-thirty-six-dramatic-situations.txt** is OCR of the 1921 James Knapp Reeve edition: body text is readable with occasional character-level OCR errors; the alphabetical index/appendix tables were dropped as unusable OCR garbage.
- **besant-james--the-art-of-fiction.txt** combines two sources (Besant's lecture from a GitHub transcription; James's essay extracted from PG #58471 *Partial Portraits*, i.e., the revised 1888 text of the 1884 essay). Provenance is noted inside the file.
- **poe--the-philosophy-of-composition.txt** was extracted from PG #55749 (*The Raven and The Philosophy of Composition*, Paul Elder & Co., 1907); the Raven poem itself and illustrations were not included.

## Notes

- **Misses: none.** All 11 targets acquired and verified against their PG title pages before stripping.
- Gatsby (PG 64317, added Jan 2021) and Mrs Dalloway (PG 71865, added Oct 2023) both turned out to be mirrored by GITenberg despite being post-2016 additions — no Standard Ebooks fallback needed.
- Heart of Darkness: PG has two editions (219 and 526); 219 (the standard one) was used.
- Maupassant: the Complete (180-story) edition was taken rather than a single volume — at 2.7 MB it fit comfortably inside the 12 MB budget and gives far better coverage of the turn-ending pattern.
- Chekhov: PG 13415 is the Constance Garnett translation (Tales of Chekhov, Vol. 3), as specified.
- Cleaning applied beyond the START/END markers: leading "Produced by ..." credits, the bracketed PG editor's note in Great Expectations, trailing "End of the Project Gutenberg EBook" lines, the transcriber's note at the end of Mrs Dalloway, and the "A Note on the Text" appendix at the end of The Age of Innocence.
