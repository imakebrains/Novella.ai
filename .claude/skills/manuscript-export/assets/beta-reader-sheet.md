# Beta reader feedback sheet

*(Send with every beta PDF. Fill in the bracketed fields, keep 3–5 questions —
more and readers skip the sheet, fewer and you learn nothing actionable.)*

---

**Book:** [TITLE] — draft of [DATE]
**Reader:** ____________  **Finished on:** ____________

Thank you for reading. Please be blunt — a kind review now costs the book a
real one later. Where you can, note a chapter or scene, not a page number
(pagination changes between drafts).

## 1. Where did you skim?

List any stretch you found yourself skipping or reading faster just to get
through. Don't explain why unless a reason jumps out — the *where* is the data.

## 2. What didn't you believe?

Any moment a character's choice, a coincidence, or a piece of the world made
you think "that wouldn't happen"? Quote or describe the moment.

## 3. Where did you stop wanting to read?

If you ever put the book down and felt no pull to pick it back up, where were
you? (If you never did, say so — that's data too.)

## 4. What did you think was going to happen?

At the halfway point, what did you expect the ending to be? (This shows what
the book was promising, whether or not it meant to.)

## 5. What stuck?

One scene, line, or character you'd still remember in a month.
