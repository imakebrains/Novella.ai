# Novel Checkpoint — the 5-chapter quick audit

Small, frequent audits beat heroic late ones. This is the quick pass; the full
seven-audit battery belongs at act boundaries (see novel-revision).

## 1. Reverse-outline scan (scene-functions.md, last ~5 chapters)

- Any row where Change = "—" or restates a prior change? Two adjacent = high severity
- Same character pair having the same conversation without escalation?
- Any principal character absent longer than ~5 chapters?
- Do the Conflict entries rise across the stretch, or plateau?

## 2. Promise/payoff scan (promise-ledger.md, whole book)

- **Orphans:** open promises untouched 15+ chapters → reinforce soon, schedule the
  payoff, or mark abandoned-intentional with a why
- **Droughts:** 5+ recent chapters planting nothing new (early-book droughts are
  the dangerous ones)
- Major promises touched within the last ~5 chapters? "Touched" = reinforced or
  complicated, not restated

## 3. Waveform check (reader-experience.md, last ~5 rows)

- Same dominant feeling 4+ chapters running?
- Any "question alive in the reader's mind" cells empty?
- Sustained dread/grief without a relief valve?

## 4. Story graph (if graphify-novel is installed)

Update the graph with the new chapters; run a status query for open threads and
dormant-past-wake-date items.

## 5. Triage and log

Findings table → two piles: **fix now** (knowledge errors, flat adjacent scenes —
cheap while fresh) vs. **park for revision** (structural, needs the act's shape).
Log the checkpoint + fix list in `session-log.md`, then commit.

Do not slide into revision here. The checkpoint protects momentum; it doesn't
replace drafting with polishing.
