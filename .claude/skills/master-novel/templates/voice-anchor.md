## Interiority depth
Example: close but withheld — Mara notices, rarely explains what noticing costs her.

## Metaphor domains
Example: tide, rope, salvage, weather. Never machinery, never sports.

## Lexical tics
Example: "against need", concrete nouns over abstractions, contractions in narration.

## What this narrator never does
Example: never editorializes about a character's morality; never opens a scene with weather.
