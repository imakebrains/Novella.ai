## 1. The diagnosis, in one page

The author's three complaints are one problem with three symptoms.

1. **"No personal connection."** Verified, and localized: across ~108,000
   words of manuscript there is exactly one sustained self-disclosure — the
   Dedication — and zero personal stories inside any chapter. The Dedication
   writes a check the chapters never cash. Attachment is reciprocal; a reader
   will not hand their heart to a narrator who never hands over his own.
2. **"Not emotionally perceptive."** Half wrong. The perception is present
   and sometimes excellent (the discovered-lie passage, the cynicism passage,
   the Pilate scene, the divided-prayer diagnosis). What is missing is
   *presence*: perception without self-disclosure eventually feels like
   surveillance. The book sees the reader and refuses to be seen.
3. **"Repetitive without being repetitive."** Confirmed and named: the prose
   repeats **shapes**, not words — six recurring sentence skeletons, one
   twelve-chapter macro-template in V2, one argumentative move performed
   seven times, five ideas re-argued across chapters. The ear counts shapes.

**Root cause, once:** the book teaches *at* the reader from behind glass,
in recurring shapes, with no story engine pulling forward. Every fix below
is one of three motions: put the author on the page, give each idea one
home, and make each chapter run a story engine.

## 2. The five convergent findings

Every lens — both comp books, both audits, both research tracks, the
publisher views — independently converged on these:

1. **The author must go first.** Vulnerability before authority; wounds shown
   before balm (Lewis's *A Grief Observed* out-persuades *The Problem of
   Pain* on the same subject); never ask the reader for an honesty not
   already performed on the page.
2. **Write to one reader — the author's younger self at his deepest doubt.**
   Categories exclude; one rendered person includes. Every chapter is tested
   against "would he have kept reading?"
3. **Scene first, sentence second.** Dramatize before exhorting; aphorisms
   land at structural boundaries after staged experience; decisive moments
   are scenes, summary lives at the borders. The manuscript's own prose
   breaks down exactly where a story is being avoided.
4. **The repetition is shape-level; the cure is rationing plus one home per
   idea.** Deliberate refrains survive only as escalating callbacks under
   numeric budgets (see §7).
5. **Nonfiction chapters must run a story engine.** A promise on page one,
   an open loop held to the chapter's end, gold coins mid-argument, planted
   payoffs, and no chapter ending at emotional zero. Supporting consensus:
   certify change through the hostile witness; anchor doctrine in totemic
   objects.

## 3. The chapter architecture

The template (adapted from the craft research for this book's fixed ritual —
scale to a 4,000–5,500-word chapter):

| # | Beat | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Cold open in scene** (500–1,000 w) | A particular moment, mid-motion — the author's own room where possible, a dramatized biblical room otherwise (the Pilate standard). No thesis first. |
| 2 | **The turn / open loop** (100–300 w) | The scene poses the chapter's question concretely and is paused before resolution. |
| 3 | **Ascend to the idea** (700–1,200 w) | Name the question generally; think on the page; steelman the strongest objection before answering it. |
| 4 | **Argument with gold coins** (1,200–2,000 w) | Scripture, reasoning, ONE word study (vary the entrance). A vivid image, moment, or held silence every few pages. Section breaks land leaning forward. |
| 5 | **The vulnerability beat** (250–600 w) | The author's specific, costly, resolved disclosure, placed where the argument needs a human warrant. Confession precedes the chapter's demand on the reader. |
| 6 | **Close the loop** (300–700 w) | Return to the opening scene; it must mean more now. The final body line leans into the next chapter's question. |
| 7 | **Reasoning Reflection** (verse / ponder) | Same voice, quieter — a diminuendo, not a genre switch. Two singular-"you" questions that harvest what the chapter planted. |
| 8 | **Prayer** (75–150 w) | Chapter-specific, echoing its images and doubts. The last callback. Land the plane once. |

**Resolutions of the packet's contradictions:**

- *Forward lean vs. "land the plane once."* Both. The last line of body prose
  (beat 6) leans forward; the Reflection and prayer descend. The chapter ends
  settled in spirit and unfinished in question.
- *Fixed template vs. template drone.* The skeleton is fixed **deep**, varied
  **at the surface**: the Reasoning Reflection is the book's one visible
  ritual; openings rotate (author's scene, biblical scene, reader's moment);
  closing charges vary in form (a story, a single line, an image); the
  For-the-Skeptic/For-the-Believer slots dissolve into the body.
- *Self-conviction vs. interrogation.* The book stays self-convicting — but
  conviction is **sequenced** (author confesses first) and **derived** (from
  the reader's own premises), with one held question instead of a battery.
- *Disclosure floor.* Specificity of feeling and cost is mandatory;
  identifying detail is optional. Veiled and composite stories are permitted
  under the ethics rules in `notes/story-inventory.md`. Scars, not wounds.

## 4. Whole-book architecture

Working from V2's fifteen chapters and the current master's rewrite:

1. **Rebuild the Introduction** (currently missing from the master). Keep
   V2's host posture ("this book is not written to mock your questions");
   cut the reader taxonomies; seat it on the fuller version of the
   Dedication's story, told once, that later chapters reference in
   installments. End it on the honest question the book exists to answer,
   visibly unanswered.
2. **Release the Dedication in installments.** The suppressed spine of the
   book, distributed at each chapter's point of highest demand: the wrong
   voice let past the gate (Ch. 1) → the divided prayer (Ch. 2) → the comfort
   that was never true (Ch. 3) → the silence season (the suffering/doubt
   chapters) → the transformed life (the witness chapter, where V2's own
   thesis — *I know because I have seen* — finally gets its exhibit).
3. **One home per idea** (from the repetition audit, adopted wholesale):
   "everyone trusts something" lives in Ch. 2; atheism's burden of proof
   with it; the moral argument wholly in Ch. 6, freeing Ch. 5 to be the
   pastoral suffering chapter; Romans 1:20 and *kathorao* move to the
   creation chapter; argument-vs-encounter crescendos once, in Ch. 14;
   "nothing is neutral" belongs to Ch. 1 and is thereafter assumed.
4. **Merge the moral material of V2 Ch. 5–6**; Ch. 5 promises the trap
   ("we will spring it in the next chapter"), Ch. 6 springs it.
5. **Name the book's signature move once** — in Ch. 1 or the Introduction:
   *this book asks what every objection must borrow in order to object.*
   Then each chapter performs a variation instead of re-performing the
   same three beats incognito seven times.
6. **The question chain.** Sequence chapters so each closing question is the
   next chapter's opening pressure (argument as plot): Who has been forming
   my mind? → Can faith survive examination? → Is there a truth to examine?
   → Does the universe testify? → Then why does it hurt? → Why does *wrong*
   exist at all? → … → and the last chapter reframes: the book that appeared
   to be about arguments was about recovering a first love (the Revelation
   2:4 shape — seeded from the Introduction onward, a reframe, never a
   rug-pull).
7. **Motifs.** Choose two or three totemic anchors and run them the length
   of the book with escalating meaning. Strong candidates already native to
   the manuscript: **the gate** (Ch. 1's guarded heart, returning as the
   door of obedience Pilate walked away from, returning again at the final
   invitation), **the fire** (the Dedication's closing image — some fires
   reveal what was never meant to burn — returning at the cost-of-truth and
   witness chapters), and **the room** (Pilate's chamber; the reader's own
   quiet room where the questions live; the room where the author stopped
   pretending). Plant in the first third, pay off in the last.

## 5. Positioning (the publisher lens on this book)

- **Felt need, one sentence:** *for the person whose faith is quietly coming
  apart under questions they are ashamed to say out loud — who suspects
  belief cannot survive honest thinking — this book reasons with them all
  the way down, and goes first.*
- **Shelf:** apologetics–discipleship hybrid (thinking reader's devotional
  spine: Reasoning Reflection), nearest neighbors *The Reason for God*
  (steelmanned doubts), *Disappointment with God* (the unshockable
  confessor), *Crazy Love* (self-implicated urgency), *Gentle and Lowly*
  (devotional recursion, and its warning about padding).
- **The handle:** the book needs one nameable framework a reader can carry
  and repeat. Candidates to develop, all already latent in the manuscript:
  **"Every mind is being discipled"** (the anchor line — arguably the
  handle), **"Guard the gate"** (the practice), **"What must unbelief
  borrow?"** (the method). Pick one as the spine phrase; let the others
  serve it.
- **Authority in-text:** the author's credential in this genre is the
  survived season plus the honest reasoning, established by exhibit (the
  installments), not by biography page. Falsifiable specificity is the
  currency.
- **Title:** *Reasoning Faith* holds; the subtitle should carry the felt
  need rather than the category (current: "A Spirit-Filled Case for Truth in
  a World of Doubt" — consider testing variants that name the reader's
  ache, e.g. the questions asked alone at night, once the Introduction is
  rebuilt and the book's promise is exact).

## 6. The one-reader dossier

The interview's decision: the book is written first to the author's younger
self at the point of deepest doubt — who is simultaneously the skeptic, the
wounded, and the drifting believer. Before Stage 1 below, fill out the
private dossier (keep it in Drive, not this public repo): the season and
month; what he was consuming daily; the exact phrasing of the thoughts he
would not say aloud; what well-meaning church answers made it worse; what he
would have needed to read to stay one more week; what would have made him
close the book (mockery, categories, unearned certainty, a lecture with no
person in it). **Every chapter is tested against that last list.**

## 7. Numeric budgets (binding, per the repetition audit)

| Device | Budget |
| --- | --- |
| Not-ladder ("Not X. Not Y. Z.") | ≤ 3 in the whole book, at load-bearing peaks (keep Ch. 4's) |
| "X is not Y. It is Z." definitions | ≤ 1 per chapter |
| Anaphora catalogs / parallel stacks | ≤ 1 deliberate hammer per section; none as paragraph engine |
| Display aphorisms (boxed/centered) | ≤ 3 per chapter |
| ◆ ◆ ◆ dividers | 1–2 per chapter, at genuine hinges |
| Greek/Hebrew word studies | 1 per chapter, entrance varied |
| Lists | 1 per chapter, and only if it is a tool, not a rhythm |
| Question strings | Never 3+ unanswered in a row; one held question preferred |
| One-line paragraphs | A handful per chapter, each spent at a real peak |
| "One of the greatest X…" openers, "That changes everything" landings | Retire as defaults; each use deliberate |
| Sentence profile | Middle register 15–25 words dominant; short = event; 35+ words = deliberate crescendo, splices repaired |

## 8. Revision sequence and acceptance criteria

**Stage 0 — Story harvest (before touching prose).** Fill the private story
inventory (`notes/story-inventory.md` protocol). *Done when:* each drafted
and planned chapter has at least one true story assigned to its vulnerability
beat and one candidate cold open, each marked full / veiled / composite.

**Stage 1 — Structure.** Rebuild the Introduction; fix idea homes; merge V2
Ch. 5–6 material; name the signature move; lay the question chain and motif
plan across the TOC. *Done when:* a one-line-per-chapter reverse outline
shows no idea argued twice and every chapter ending leaning into the next.

**Stage 2 — Chapter rebuilds (Ch. 1 first).** Apply the eight-beat template
chapter by chapter: cold open, loop, confession-before-charge, gold coins,
bookend close, chapter-specific Reflection and prayer. Chapter 1 needs the
deepest rework (lecture posture intact from V2; run-ons; the "my faith"
slip). *Done when:* each chapter passes the presence check (`voice/`, §6)
and the 12-point craft checklist (`research/craft-research…`, §6.2).

**Stage 3 — Line pass.** The budgets in §7, both directions of the cadence
fix (fuse V2 fragments; split master clause-chains), retire default
connective tissue, verify KJV wording and word studies. *Done when:* a
read-aloud of each chapter finds no three consecutive sentences sharing a
shape and no page with more than one parallel stack.

**Stage 4 — Cold read.** Read the whole book as the one reader, asking only
"where would he have put it down?" Fix those pages. Then beta readers, then
export via the existing skills pipeline.

**Order matters:** structure before stories before lines. Never line-edit a
section that might still move (the `novel-revision` rule applies to
nonfiction unchanged).

## 9. What is already working (protect it)

The Ch. 1–3 rewrite broke V2's aphorism-box drone; Ch. 2's divided-faith
center and Ch. 3 nearly whole are the book's proof of capability; the
Dedication is braver in the rewrite than in V2; the discernment checklist in
Ch. 1 earns its list format; the Pilate scene is the model for every scene
the book still needs. Salvage list from V2 (per the emotional audit §7):
the Introduction's host posture, Ch. 5's two-problems-of-evil frame, Ch. 7's
silence-of-God interiority, Ch. 12's beauty images, Ch. 13's
testimony-vs-performance distinction — each rebuilt into the new
architecture, not pasted.
