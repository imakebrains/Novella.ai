# Reasoning Faith — Book Workspace

This folder is the dedicated workspace for Drew Peckham's book **Reasoning Faith:
A Spirit-Filled Case for Truth in a World of Doubt**. It is deliberately separated
from the Novella.ai application code and from the general `writing-skills/` library.
Nothing in here is app code; nothing outside this folder is book material.

## Important: what lives here and what does not

This repository is **public**. For that reason:

- **Manuscript text does NOT live here.** The working masters stay in Google Drive
  (`MASTER_Reasoning Faith` is the current rewrite; `Reasoning Faith - MASTER V2`
  is the previous full manuscript). See `manuscript/README.md`.
- Analyses may contain **brief quotations** for commentary and craft study only.
- No personal or third-party private material is stored here.

## The current problem statement (2026-08-17)

The book has strong content but is not landing emotionally:

1. **Personal connection** — the reader is not attached to the author or to a
   human story; the material teaches but does not befriend.
2. **Emotional perception** — the prose argues more than it feels; it is not
   driving the reader forward the way story does.
3. **Repetition** — recurring sentence shapes and restated points ("repetitive
   without being repetitive").

The work in this folder exists to fix those three things while keeping the
book's doctrinal spine intact (KJV Scripture, word studies, Reasoning
Reflection sections, the Chesterton/Pascal/Tozer fused voice).
