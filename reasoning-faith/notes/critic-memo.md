## 1. Contradictions the synthesis must resolve

1. **Humor transfer vs. the author's veto.** `first-love-effect-voice-and-personality.md`
   §7.1(8) says the comic register "transfers intact"; the publisher view (§6.2) leans the
   same way. The interview (§4) rules: "The fiction voice stays out. Completely... Not
   through humor." Resolution path: keep the *function* of the humor (defenses lowered,
   credibility banked by the author going first) but deliver it through confession,
   self-implication, and emotional precision — the devices the interview permits.
2. **Was the novel's twist seeded?** Story engine §6: "the plant density is high enough
   that the twist feels revealed, not imposed." Publisher view §5.1: "the twist arrives
   essentially unseeded... no dream-logic tells." Opposite verdicts on the same manuscript.
   The derived rule both share (a final reframe must be seeded from page one) survives
   either way, but the synthesis should not cite the novel as proof of successful seeding.
3. **How undisguised must Drew's stories be?** The emotional audit repeatedly instructs
   "take off the disguise" (§3c) and "take off the 'you'" (§6, Ch. 3), and infers the
   real person behind the Dedication as the Ch. 2 story candidate. The interview (§2)
   allows veiled/composite stories and states "no obligation to expose anything raw."
   Resolution: specificity of *feeling and cost* is mandatory; identifying *detail* is
   negotiable. Define that disclosure floor explicitly.
4. **"Never end at emotional zero" vs. "land the plane once."** Story engine #6 wants
   forward-leaning endings; craft research §5.2 and the standing constraint (Reasoning
   Reflection closes every chapter) require a settled landing. Resolve by locating the
   forward lean: last line of body prose opens the next chapter's question; the
   Reflection descends as diminuendo; the prayer is a callback, not a summary.
5. **Fixed chapter template vs. template-induced drone.** Craft research §6.1 prescribes
   a repeatable 8-beat chapter shape; the repetition audit (fix #1) diagnosed V2's fixed
   slots as the deepest repetition problem and prescribes exactly one fixed ritual.
   Resolve: fixed deep skeleton, varied surface — openings and closings vary in *form*
   while the beat logic stays constant; Reasoning Reflection is the single ritual.
6. **How to open the book.** FLE voice §7.1(1) and story engine #1 endorse a confessional
   essay prologue (no scene); publisher view flags that same prologue as 1,300 words of
   telling; craft research demands a cold open in scene. Resolve: stage the confession
   *as* a scene — a particular night, a particular room — not as essay.
7. **Emotion-naming redundancy.** Story engine §3 calls it "attachment glue... a dial";
   publisher view calls it a defect needing a 15–20 percent cut. Set the nonfiction dial:
   name each feeling once, at the structural boundary, after the scene has earned it.
8. **Self-convicting register vs. the interrogation finding.** The interview wants the
   reader to "analyze themselves through the Word"; the emotional audit shows stacked
   questions read as a security checkpoint (§3a). Resolve by sequence: the author's
   confession precedes every demand — conviction is invited, not conducted.
9. **Minor factual mismatches** (note once, no action): novel word count ~104,000 vs.
   ~104,500; voice report reads the unpunctuated final line as source-state while the
   publisher view calls the file complete.

## 4. The five load-bearing convergent findings

1. **The author must go first.** Zero personal narrative in ~108k words is the root
   diagnosis; vulnerability-before-authority is confirmed by all three FLE reports, both
   research files (Nouwen, Brown, the Grief Observed contrast), and the audit's rule:
   never ask the reader for an honesty not already performed on the page.
2. **Write to one reader — Drew's younger self.** One-heart audit finding, Steinbeck/
   Vonnegut/King, the "written for me" test, niche-flag marketing logic, and the
   interview's explicit decision all agree: categories exclude, a person includes.
3. **Scene first, sentence second.** Dramatize before exhorting; aphorisms land only at
   structural boundaries after staged experience; the Pilate scene is the in-book proof;
   the ladder of abstraction is the operating manual; prose breaks down exactly where a
   story is being avoided.
4. **The repetition is shape-level, and the cure is rationing plus one home per idea.**
   Six sentence skeletons and one macro-template, not repeated words; parallel-stack
   fatigue confirmed independently by craft research; the novel exhibits the same flaw;
   deliberate refrains must escalate (devotional recursion) or die.
5. **Run a story engine through nonfiction chapters.** Page-one promise, open loop held
   to chapter's end, gold coins mid-argument, planted payoffs and motif callbacks, no
   dead-zero endings (as resolved in §1.4), title thesis cashed at the close as an
   earned reframe.

Near-misses worth carrying: certify change through the hostile witness / steelman the
skeptic (five reports); totemic objects as theology anchors (three reports).
